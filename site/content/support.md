Bug reports, suggestions and development all happen in the open on GitHub.

## Report a bug

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/zzoko/PerformanceHUD/issues/new). These details make a problem much quicker to track down:

- Your Mac model and macOS version
- Your PerformanceHUD version (shown in the menu)
- The game or app you were using, and whether it ran windowed, borderless or fullscreen
- What you expected to see, and what happened instead

## Suggest a feature

Suggestions go in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/zzoko/PerformanceHUD/issues) too. Check the open issues first, in case someone has already asked for it.

## Source code and contributions

The source code, version history and pull requests live in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/zzoko/PerformanceHUD). Contributions are subject to the [license](/license/).
